The Premise

Real Relationships. Zero Pitches.

77% of retail sales happen in stores, but brands get almost no customer data. We're bringing together the people building physical brands to talk about what actually works.

01

The Retail Blind Spot

How brands use packaging and store displays to capture direct customer data at the shelf.

02

Closed-Door Honesty

100% off-the-record. Candid talk about slotting fees, retailer margins, and rising ad costs.

03

The Packaging Shift

How the 2026 GS1 2D barcode transition lets brands connect packs to instant mobile checkout.
