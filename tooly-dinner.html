The Concept

Why a Dinner Beats 100 Cold Calls

Real trust doesn't start in software demos. It happens around good food and candid conversation.

01 / The Room

Real Operators Only

Founders and COOs running 15 to 80-person service businesses with real payroll, active clients, and complex operations.

02 / Ground Rule

Zero Pitching

No sponsor slides. No software demos. Just open discussion about what breaks when you scale and how people fix it.

03 / Depth

Unhurried Attention

Three uninterrupted hours to talk systems, margins, tool sprawl, and operational problems you don't share publicly.

Schedule
