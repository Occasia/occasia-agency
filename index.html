The Turnkey Engine

The 3-Step Client Acquisition System

We don't just give you a checklist. We build, manage, and install the complete system directly into your business.

01

Precision Sourcing & LinkedIn Outbound

We identify your exact ICP (target industries, revenue benchmarks, and executive titles like CEO, VP, CMO). We execute personal, conversational LinkedIn outreach inviting them to a private executive roundtable—never a sales pitch.

  • Verified ICP filtering & enrichment
  • High-status, zero-pitch invitation copy
  • 100% attendance qualification & RSVP tracking
02

The Curated Executive Dinner

We handle the venue booking, private dining room selection, tasting menus, and run-of-show. You and your team sit at the head of the table as hosts, leading structured discussions that surface real executive challenges.

  • 10–15 vetted decision-makers in a private room
  • Turnkey venue coordination & hospitality
  • Facilitated discussion prompts that highlight your expertise
03

Post-Dinner Pipeline Conversion

The dinner creates genuine trust; the follow-up framework closes the deal. We install our 48-hour follow-up playbook to transition dinner conversations into 1-on-1 strategy sessions, pilot scopes, and closed contracts.

  • Proven 48-hour post-dinner outreach scripts
  • Seamless bridge from dinner discussion to discovery call
  • Compressed enterprise sales cycles (weeks vs months)
