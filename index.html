The Acquisition Engine

The 5-Step Acquisition System

From account mapping and prospect validation to private room production and closed pipeline.

STEP 01 · ACCOUNT MAPPING

Sourcing 200+ Potential Customers

We map out a custom database of 200+ target accounts based on your exact ACV, target industry verticals, and ideal customer profile (ICP).

  • Exact VP & C-Suite decision-maker mapping
  • Zero broad or purchased email lists
  • Pre-screened for purchasing authority and budget
STEP 02 · CLIENT APPROVAL

Prospect Validation

You review and approve the curated prospect pool before a single outreach message is sent. You have 100% sign-off authority.

  • Complete visibility on names, titles & companies
  • 100% veto authority: decline any unaligned accounts
  • Guarantees zero seat-fillers or irrelevant attendees
STEP 03 · PEER-LEVEL REACH

Outreach and Curation

We execute personalized, high-status invitations to approved leaders via LinkedIn and direct executive channels.

  • 100% pitch-free invitation copy framed as a peer salon
  • Peer-to-peer positioning that executives actually accept
  • 12–15 confirmed executive RSVPs per dining table
STEP 04 · HOSPITALITY & STAGE

Event Planning & Logistics

We manage the entire evening end-to-end so your team simply shows up and builds peer-level trust with your target buyers.

  • Private dining room in top venues (Montreal / Toronto)
  • Full hospitality, sommelier wine pairing & on-site host
  • Roundtable discussion prompts positioning your authority
STEP 05 · DEAL ACCELERATION

Post-Dinner Conversion

We execute structured 48-hour follow-up playbooks to turn dinner conversations into warm pipeline and signed high-ticket contracts.

  • Structured 48-hour follow-up playbooks & conversion bridges
  • Attendee intelligence notes detailing prospect pain points
  • Warm transitions into 1-on-1 discovery calls and proposals
