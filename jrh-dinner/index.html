The Strategic Reality

Traditional Search BD vs. Private Dinner

Cold outreach and noisy conferences don't win C-suite search mandates. This model gives you direct control over your word-of-mouth.

✕ Traditional Search Outreach

Cold calls, noise & passive referrals

  • ✕
    Industry Mixers & Events: High sponsorship costs to talk to people with no hiring authority.
  • ✕
    Cold Outreach: Zero CEO or CFO hands a confidential C-suite search to a cold email.
  • ✕
    Passive Word-of-Mouth: Waiting for past clients to refer new searches with no control on timing.
  • ✕
    Contingency Bidding Wars: Competing against 5 agencies on speed and fee discounts.
  • ✕
    Zero Intimacy: Impossible to discuss sensitive succession or leadership gaps in public.

✓ The Occasia × JRH Model

Proactive control of target accounts & referrals

  • ✓
    12 to 14 qualified CEOs, CFOs & COOs seated at your private dining table.
  • ✓
    4 hours of undivided attention, with zero noise and zero competitors.
  • ✓
    Referral Control: Word-of-mouth turned into a proactive engine (25–30 peer introductions).
  • ✓
    Trusted Peer Authority: Positioned as a fellow CPA, CA & Managing Partner, never a vendor.
  • ✓
    Searches Initiated on Trust: Confidential C-suite and finance mandates started naturally.
